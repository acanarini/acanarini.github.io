PI

Dr. Alberto Canarini

Associate Professor, Department of Biological, Geological and Environmental Sciences, University of Bologna. The main goal of my research is to understand the effect of changing environmental conditions on fundamental biogeochemical mechanisms that control the fate of important elements in terrestrial ecosystems in which microbes are involved, with an emphasis on the carbon cycling.

  • (2017) Ph.D. Soil Biogeochemistry, the University of Sydney (Australia)
  • (2011) M.S. Ecology and Evolution, University of Ferrara (Italy)
  • (2009) B.S. Biology, University of Bologna (Italy)

Postdocs

Arun Kumar Devarajan

Postdoc, started July 2025. My research interests focus on understanding how climate change and environmental stressors regulate microbial responses and their feedbacks to ecosystem processes. My PhD research investigated how warming magnitude and duration alter bacterial community structure and nitrogen cycling potential. Currently, as a postdoctoral researcher in the ERC project EcoMEMO at the University of Bologna, I study how precipitation history shapes soil microbial drought responses and their biogeochemical cycling potential in Mediterranean ecosystems.

  • (2024) Ph.D. Microbiology, University of Tartu (Estonia)
  • (2019) M.S. Agricultural Microbiology, Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (India)

Prof. Feike A. Dijkstra

Visiting Professor, started July 2026. Feike is a Professor at the University of Sydney and leads its Terrestrial Biogeochemistry Laboratory. His research examines how climate and land-use change affect carbon, nutrient and water dynamics in terrestrial ecosystems, with a focus on plant–microbe–soil interactions in grasslands and agroecosystems using field, laboratory and stable-isotope experiments.
